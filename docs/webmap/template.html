    How the kilns were found. YOLO11 detector on Sentinel-2 winter composites, filtered by land cover, kept only if hot in ≥12% of clear images, with airports, refineries and year-round heat sources removed. A visual check of 25 random kilns found 21 of 23 working (precision 0.91, 95% CI 0.73–0.98).
