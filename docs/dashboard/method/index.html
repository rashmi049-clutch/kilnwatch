Method and accuracy

How the numbers were produced, how they were checked, and what they cannot tell you. Full report, code and processing parameters: GitHub.

Pipeline
  1. 1.Detect. A YOLO11 oriented-box detector, fine-tuned on a public kiln dataset, run over Sentinel-2 imagery (10 m) of the whole NCR, 2023–24 season.
  2. 2.Confirm firing. A shortwave-infrared heat test on every clear dry-season Sentinel-2 scene keeps only sites that were actually hot (firing) in 2023–24.
  3. 3.Check by eye. Every remaining kiln was checked on high-resolution Esri imagery; doubtful ones were judged by the analyst in Google Earth history. Parks, stadiums and truck yards that passed as kilns were removed.
  4. 4.Count exposure. 800 m rings (CPCB siting distance) around each kiln; children 5–14 from WorldPop 2020 counted once inside the union of rings; schools from the UDISE+ register.
  5. 5.Test closures. Each kiln's 2025–26 status from the same heat test, compared between kilns with and without a school within 800 m.

School locations

School points come from the official UDISE+ register (Ministry of Education / NIC SchoolGIS). They are not field-verified. Before counting, points were dropped if they fell in the wrong district, more than 300 m from built-up land, more than 50 m from any building, or stacked on schools of other villages, plus points the analyst reported as wrong. An ongoing analyst check compares register points with named map listings; results will be added here.

Emissions (estimate)

Season PM2.5 from all 3,057 kilns: about 4,402 t (low (zigzag, 4 M bricks)) to 11,555 t (high (fixed chimney, 6 M bricks)). Emission factors from published kiln measurements; production per kiln is assumed, not measured.

Limitations
  • School register locations can be tens to hundreds of metres off; some real schools near kilns are missing from the register, so school counts are a minimum.
  • WorldPop is a modelled population surface (2020), not a census count.
  • 800 m is a siting distance, not a health threshold; living inside it does not measure dose.
  • Firing status comes from heat detected in clear Sentinel-2 scenes; small or intermittent firing can be missed.
